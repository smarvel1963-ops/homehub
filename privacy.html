Home Hub privacy policy

Home Hub is an app by Marvel Corp, part of the Hub family of apps. Last updated October 22, 2026.

The short version

What you type into Home Hub stays on your phone. We have no Home Hub account system and no database of your home. There are no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. A few features you choose to use send data to other services, and each one is listed below.

What stays on your phone

Your home's rooms, outdoor spaces and systems, the items, tasks and notes in each one, your Hub Predicted Value details, your to-dos, lists, bills, reminders and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see them. If other Hub apps are on the same phone, Home Hub can show a short read-only summary from them (and they from Home Hub); that happens on the phone and nothing is sent to us. You can erase everything any time in ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase everything. Erase also signs the phone out of Google, tells Google to drop that sign-in, and forgets saved purchase emails and the AI passphrase for every Hub app on the phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data also removes everything.

Google user data

Home Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features you turned on, in the app, on your phone. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and no person at Marvel Corp reads it. To disconnect, use ⚙ Settings in Home Hub or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup copy can be deleted from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Home value

Hub Predicted Value is an estimate from public home-sales data, not an appraisal. Your home details are not shared with real-estate agents, lenders or anyone else.

Children

Home Hub is for adults (18 and older). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Selling data

We do not sell or rent personal information. We hold almost none of it.

Changes

If what Home Hub sends anywhere changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
